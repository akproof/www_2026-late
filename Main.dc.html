Questions we hear often

Who do you work with?

Central banks and regulators, banks and payment firms, digital asset issuers, trade and logistics firms, public agencies, schools and event organisers, and service, facility and environmental operators.

Can you name your clients?

We keep client names confidential and describe our work by industry, type of work and impact. References are available on request.

How do ATNet and AFWork differ?

ATNet is infrastructure for verifiable digital identity and data exchange. AFWork runs teams of AI agents toward a defined mission, built to be safe, reliable, accountable and private.

Can you take a pilot into production?

Yes. Our teams work from regulation to architecture to production, and from sandbox to live system.

Where are you based?

Dubai, Hamburg, Hong Kong, London, New York, Nicosia, Rotterdam, San Francisco, and Singapore.

Do you offer training?

Yes. We run courses, workshops and mentorship for students and professionals through three programmes: Enrichment, Advanced Learning and Specialisation.
