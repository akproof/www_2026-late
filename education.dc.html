About the programme
1

Enrichment

For beginners entering the 21st century digital economy through the fundamental concepts of blockchain and cryptography. It covers the ideas needed to appreciate what blockchain technology is, and the vital role that solutions built on it play in regulated web3 infrastructure.

What students learn

Most students
Explain what trust is, why it is the cornerstone of all economic activity, and how it supports economic activity online and in real life.
Some students
Explain the importance of cryptography, game theory and incentives in digital economies.
The most advanced students
Explain cryptography concepts.
2

Advanced Learning

For learners seeking deeper theoretical knowledge of the infrastructure behind the 21st century digital economy, through advanced concepts in blockchain and cryptography. It covers blockchain technology in the depth needed to fully appreciate the solutions built on it and their vital role in regulated web3 infrastructure.

What students learn

Most students
Explain why blockchain is important and what it is.
Some students
Explain how blockchains work, including their cryptographic concepts, and how blockchain applies to different use cases.
The most advanced students
Discuss blockchain case studies on identity and privacy.
3

Specialisation

For learners ready to apply their new skills to applications for the 21st century digital economy, through a practical design project, a presentation and symposium, and a practicum with industry professionals. It covers current events and trends from working practitioners, to support the design and building of a regulated web3 application.

What students learn

All students
Become familiar with design thinking and project management.
Most students
Apply the Trust over IP (ToIP) or other web3 models, the GDPR or other privacy regulations, and the PCTF or other trust frameworks.
The most advanced students
Become familiar with Ethereum, Hyperledger Fabric, Quorum and R3 Corda.
