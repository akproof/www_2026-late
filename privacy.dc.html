2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit akproof.com or contact us through it, why, and what rights you have.

It does not cover our client work, which our contracts govern, or job applications, which have their own policy. Other websites we link to have their own policies.

3. Information we collect

We collect only what you send us and what is needed to run and measure the site. We do not ask for sensitive data; please don’t include it in a message.

Source What we receive When
Contact form Your name, work email, organisation (optional) and message, and the page you sent it from. A hidden field that only automated spam fills in. When you send the form
Email Your address, what you write and anything in your signature When you email us
Visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) The page visited, the site that referred you, your country, browser and device type, and page load timings. No cookies and no identifier that follows you across sites. Each page view
Hosting and security logs (GitHub, Cloudflare) Your IP address, browser details, the pages requested and the time Each request to the site
Browser storage One item, akp-privacy, recording that you saw our privacy notice and any choices you made there When you close or answer the notice

Cloudflare receives your IP address to deliver the statistics. For more information, see Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

4. How we use it and our legal basis

We use your data only for the purposes below. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, build profiles, or make automated decisions about you.

Purpose Data used Legal basis under the EU and UK GDPR
Reply to your enquiry and follow it up Contact form, email Our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us (Art. 6(1)(f)); where you ask about our services, steps you request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Understand how the site is used, in total Visit statistics Our legitimate interest in running and improving the site (Art. 6(1)(f)). Nothing is stored on or read from your device for this.
Keep the site secure and available Hosting and security logs Our legitimate interest in protecting the site and its visitors (Art. 6(1)(f))
Remember your privacy choices Browser storage Strictly necessary to respect your choice; our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c))
Meet legal duties and handle claims Any of the above, as needed Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)); legitimate interest in defending claims (Art. 6(1)(f))

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object at any time (section 9).

6. International transfers

Some of our providers and offices are outside the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK. When we transfer personal data from the EEA or UK to a country without an adequacy decision, we use one of these safeguards:

  • the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, for US providers certified under it
  • the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, with the UK addendum where needed

You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards (section 11).

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as the purpose needs, then delete or anonymise it.

Data How long
Enquiries (form and email) 27 months after our last exchange; longer if the enquiry leads to a contract, under that contract’s terms
Visit statistics 6 months, with unsampled data kept for 7 days before aggregating to roughly 10%
Hosting and security logs 90 days typically, up to 180 days
Privacy notice choice (browser storage) Up to 180 days, or until you clear your browser storage

8. Cookies and similar technologies

akproof.com sets no cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking.

  • Browser storage: one item, akp-privacy, remembers that you saw our privacy notice. It is strictly necessary and is kept for up to 180 days.
  • Security cookies: if Cloudflare’s bot protection is on, Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies (such as __cf_bm) to tell people from automated traffic.
  • Optional cookies: if we ever add any, we will ask first. Reject all will be as easy as Accept all, every optional box will start unticked, and you can change your choice at any time under Privacy choices at the bottom of every page.

9. Your rights

To use any of these rights, email us (section 11). We may need to confirm your identity first, and we reply within one month, or tell you why we need longer.

In the EEA and the UK you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access)
  • correct it, or delete it
  • limit how we use it
  • send it to you or another organisation in a portable format
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests (objection)
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what we did before

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example where you live or work. For AKP’s European offices these include the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Hamburg), the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (London), the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (Nicosia) and the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Rotterdam).

Elsewhere:

10. Security, children and changes

Security. The site is served only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to enquiries is limited to the people who need it, and our providers protect their systems under their own security programmes. No system is completely secure, so please don’t send confidential information through the form.

Children. The site is meant for businesses and professionals. It is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes. When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change matters to how we use your data, we will show our privacy notice again on your next visit.

For any question about this policy or your data, email privacy@akproof.com or write to Atomic Knowledge Proof Limited, at our registered office’s address.
